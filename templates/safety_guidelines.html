Safety Guidelines

Important safety instructions for handling and using fireworks

Fireworks can be dangerous. Always follow safety instructions and adult supervision is mandatory.

General Safety Rules

1

Adult Supervision Required

Children should never handle fireworks. Always have a responsible adult supervise all fireworks activities.

2

Use in Open Areas

Only use fireworks in open, outdoor spaces away from buildings, trees, dry grass, and flammable materials.

3

Keep Water Ready

Always have a bucket of water, hose, or fire extinguisher nearby before lighting any fireworks.

4

Wear Protective Gear

Wear safety glasses and protective clothing. Never wear loose-fitting clothes that could catch fire.

5

Light One at a Time

Never light multiple fireworks simultaneously. Light one firework at a time and move away quickly.

6

Never Relight Duds

If a firework doesn't light, wait at least 20 minutes before approaching. Soak it in water and dispose properly.

Product-Specific Guidelines

Sparklers

  • Hold sparklers at arm's length away from body
  • Never give sparklers to children under 12
  • Dispose in water after complete cooling

Rockets

  • Place in a stable, upright position
  • Never aim at people, buildings, or vehicles
  • Maintain minimum 30 feet distance

Fountains

  • Place on flat, non-flammable surface
  • Keep at least 20 feet away from audience
  • Do not touch until completely cool

Crackers

  • Never hold crackers in hand while lighting
  • Use long-handled lighters or safety fuses
  • Wear ear protection for loud crackers

Emergency Information

In Case of Fire

  • Call emergency services immediately: 101
  • Evacuate the area immediately
  • Do not attempt to fight large fires yourself

In Case of Injury

  • Call ambulance: 102
  • Cool burns with running water for 20 minutes
  • Seek immediate medical attention
